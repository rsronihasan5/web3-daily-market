- **Ethereum $1,682, up 3.8% in 24 hours** — outpacing BTC's bounce, with a market cap near $206 billion. Still down about 5% on the week and roughly 29% over 30 days.
- **Solana around $70.21, up about 2.0%** in 24 hours.
- **Breadth is green today:** 57–64 of the top 100 coins are up. The biggest mover among them is Derive (DRV), up about **35%**.

The context that matters more than today's green:

- **Fear & Greed is at 11 — extreme fear.** A market can bounce hard and still be scared, and that's exactly what this looks like.
- **The 30-day damage is the story.** BTC is down ~26% in a month, ETH ~29%. One green Sunday doesn't undo that.
- **The driver is the same as BTC's:** a liquidation-driven flush followed by short covering, not a new catalyst.

What I'm taking from it as a builder: bearish months are when the noise dies down and the real work gets cheaper to do — gas is low, attention is elsewhere, and shipping stands out more. The market will do what it does; my job is to keep building through it.
