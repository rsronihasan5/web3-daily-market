# Crypto market: total cap near $2.1T, fear stuck at extreme

The total crypto market cap is around **$2.09–2.13 trillion** this morning (checked 24 November, ~09:51 Dhaka / ~03:51 UTC — sources: Blockdeck, CoinGecko via Coinpric, The Block), down roughly **1.7–2.4% over 24 hours**. Bitcoin's share of the market — BTC dominance — is about **57.8%**, and the Fear & Greed Index is at **11: Extreme Fear**.

The majors, this morning:

- **Bitcoin (BTC)** — around **$61,000**, roughly flat over 24 hours, down about 5.5% over 7 days.
- **Ethereum (ETH)** — around **$1,700** (sources range $1,685–$1,720), roughly flat to slightly down over 24 hours, down about 6% over 7 days and about 24% so far in November.
- **Solana (SOL)** — around **$72**, also roughly flat on the day.

What's actually going on:

- **Bitcoin is still the market.** With dominance near 58%, alts are mostly following BTC's chop rather than moving on their own. The Altcoin Season Index reads around **40–44 out of 100** — mixed leadership, no alt season.
- **A few coins are moving anyway.** Among the top 100, HASH was up about **16.6%** and Canton about **8.4%** in the last day, while DRV was down roughly **11–13%** — the biggest loser. Most of the top 100 is within a couple of percent either way.
- **The regulatory calendar is slipping.** Work on the US Clarity Act will likely be halted until 2027, per yesterday's market roundup — the clarity builders were hoping for keeps getting pushed back. On the other side, perpetuals exchange Backpack has been approved to serve US customers, and the World Bank is experimenting with stablecoins to modernize payments.

What I'm watching, not predicting:

1. **Whether dominance keeps rising.** Rising dominance usually means money rotating out of alts and into BTC — the defensive trade.
2. **Whether ETH can hold $1,700.** It's down a quarter in November; losing this level puts the whole alt market on notice.
3. **Fear at 11.** Extreme fear has now lasted through the whole bounce. Either sentiment turns before price does, or price turns before sentiment does — one of them has to give.

No green day, no red day — just a market catching its breath while everyone waits. I check the numbers, write them down, and get back to building.
