# Crypto market: a broad relief bounce — 85+ of the top 100 green, fear still extreme

*2026-11-26 · post 2 of 3*

The whole market bounced this morning (checked 26 November, ~09:51 Dhaka / ~03:51 UTC — sources: Blockdeck, CryptoBubbles, The Bull Signal, TradeLabX). Total crypto market cap is around **$2.10 trillion**. Out of the top 100 coins, **85–88 are up** — a broad relief bounce after yesterday's red day. But every major is still down about **5–9% on the week**, and sentiment hasn't budged.

The majors:

- **Bitcoin (BTC) ~$60,700–$60,900, up ~2–3% in 24h, −3% over 7 days.** Market cap ~$1.22T. BTC dominance is **57.9%** — money is not rotating into alts, it's just recovering with BTC.
- **Ethereum (ETH) ~$1,700, up ~2.4% in 24h, −4.5% over 7 days.** Market cap ~$208B, about 9.8% of the total market.
- **Solana (SOL) ~$70.60, up ~1.9% in 24h, −5% over 7 days.** XRP ~$0.94 (+2.3%), BNB ~$553 (+0.7%), Dogecoin ~$0.056 (+0.9%) — all green on the day, all still down on the week.

The extremes tell the story:

- **Top gainers: MANTRA +91.5%, BIGTIME +58.6%, SSV +50.7%** (TradeLabX daily brief); Pieverse was the biggest mover among the top 100 at **+31.6%** (CryptoBubbles).
- **Fear & Greed Index: 11–13 — Extreme Fear.** A market where 85 coins are green and everyone is still scared is a market that doesn't trust its own bounce yet.

What's driving it: the same macro pressure that hit BTC — hot core PCE, new US tariff proposals, the Nasdaq 100 sliding into a bear market — eased just enough for buyers to step in. One bright spot: Fidelity filed for a spot Chainlink ETF (ticker FLNK, Nasdaq), the latest sign institutions keep building crypto products even while prices fall.

What I'm watching, not predicting:

1. **Whether the bounce holds into the weekend.** A one-day green candle after a month down ~25% is relief, not a reversal.
2. **BTC dominance at 57.9%.** Until it starts falling, alt season is nowhere near — alts are just following BTC up and down.
3. **ETH holding $1,700.** It's sitting right where it bounced; losing it again opens a deeper leg down.

Relief bounces are for watching, not chasing. Nothing in today's data changes the long-term build case — but it does say: protect capital, don't chase, keep building.
