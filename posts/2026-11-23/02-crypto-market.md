What today actually looks like:

- **ETH reclaimed $1,700.** Up 2.4% to about $1,718, ETH is the only major back above the level it broke down from on Friday — the strongest of the majors right now.
- **Breadth is improving.** Around **85 of the top 100 coins are up** today. The bounce is broad, not just a Bitcoin story.
- **Not every coin is bouncing.** SKY slid about 5.4% while the market rose, a reminder that the rebound is selective. Midnight (NIGHT) was the top gainer among large coins at roughly +12.8%.
- **Sentiment hasn't caught up with price.** The Fear & Greed Index reads **11–12 — Extreme Fear**. Two green days haven't convinced anyone yet.

What I'm watching, not predicting:

1. **Bitcoin dominance.** Near 59%, it tells me capital is still hiding in BTC. Alts don't get their own run until that number starts falling.
2. **ETH holding $1,700.** If it keeps the level, it's the first major showing real strength.
3. **Whether the fear index moves.** Price up, fear still extreme — that gap usually closes one way or the other, and it closes fast.

A broad bounce in extreme fear is a market that wants to believe but doesn't yet. That's a fine place to be a builder — heads down, shipping, while the market decides.
