What I'm watching, not predicting:

1. **Whether BTC dominance keeps climbing.** At 57.8% and rising, alts keep underperforming — alt season is nowhere near.
2. **ETH holding $1,650.** It is sitting right on its 24-hour low; losing it opens a deeper leg down.
3. **Volume.** Around $29–30B in BTC volume alone — this is real selling, not a thin holiday move.

Down days test the thesis. Nothing in today's data changes the long-term build case — but it does say: protect capital, don't chase, keep building.
