# The whole market is red — and fear is extreme

*2026-11-21 · post 2 of 3*

It's a sea of red today. The total crypto market cap is around **$2.02 trillion**, down roughly **5–7% in 24 hours** (sources: Blockdeck, CoinMarketCap data via Crypto Space News, checked 21 November ~09:50 Dhaka).

The snapshot:

- **Bitcoin (BTC)** — ~$58,900, down ~5% in 24h, down ~14% in 7 days. Market cap ~$1.18T.
- **Ethereum (ETH)** — ~$1,620, down ~7% in 24h, down ~16% in 7 days. Market cap ~$198B.
- **Solana (SOL)** — ~$67–69, down ~6–7% in 24h.
- **BTC dominance** — ~58%, ETH dominance ~9.8%. Bitcoin is holding its share of the market as everything falls together — this is a market-wide selloff, not a rotation into alts.
- **Fear & Greed Index** — **12, "Extreme Fear"** (was 10 yesterday). Only about 7–8 of the top 100 coins are up today.

DeFi isn't spared either: total value locked sits near **$66.7B**, with Ethereum still holding about half of it (~$34.7B), ahead of Solana (~$4.6B) and BSC (~$4.2B).

Three things this tells me as a builder:

1. **Alts are bleeding harder than BTC.** ETH down 7% vs BTC's 5% is the usual pattern in a selloff — and it's a reminder that "diversifying" into alts doesn't diversify risk when the whole market moves together.
2. **Extreme fear is a sentiment reading, not a buy signal.** It tells you how people feel, not where the bottom is. The index was already at 10 yesterday and the market fell again.
3. **Quiet days are building days.** When prices dominate the timeline, the people still shipping — bounties, tools, guides — are the ones with something to show when sentiment turns.

No predictions here. Just the numbers, checked this morning, and the habit that matters in weeks like this: keep building, keep verifying, and don't let a red portfolio make decisions for you.
